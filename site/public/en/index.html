Features

One shortcut, six modes

Correct, rewrite or translate the same text without pasting it into a chat. Example results depend on the model, and a model can be wrong — so you always see the result before it goes in.

Correct

i will send you the raport tomorow

I will send you the report tomorrow.

Improve

The meeting was long and we talked about a lot of different important things.

In a long meeting we covered many important topics.

Formal

hey, can u send me that file by tmrw?

Hello, could you please send me that file by tomorrow?

Casual

Please be advised that the report will be sent tomorrow.

I'll send the report tomorrow.

Shorter

I would just like to point out that in my opinion it might be worth reviewing this document once more.

It's worth reviewing this document again.

Translate

I'll send the agenda before the meeting.

Podeślę agendę przed spotkaniem.

Dictation test version

  1. Hold the right Ctrl

    On macOS, Fn / 🌐. Speak while the pill says “Listening…”. With Shift you dictate in your second language.

  2. Release the key

    Local Whisper or a service you configure recognizes the speech.

  3. The text lands in the field

    Where the cursor is, as if you had typed it.

Checking while you type. A badge counts detected mistakes, and underlines appear where the text field supports them.

Your data

Your text and voice, on your terms

No GramaDrama account is needed. You install the model and services yourself — on your computer, your own server or a compatible external service — and point GramaDrama to them in its settings.

Your servicesText: a model and optional LanguageTool. Recording: Whisper. Locally or on a server you choose.
  • No account, no telemetry. The app collects no statistics, and its logs do not contain the text being corrected.
  • Offline too. When the model, LanguageTool and Whisper run locally, your text and recordings stay on your computer or network. The app has no default project server.
  • You choose the service. With a remote service, its operator decides how your data is processed and stored.

FAQ

What people ask

Does it work in my app?

Compatibility depends on how the app exposes text. The browser extension handles fields on web pages. Password fields, terminals and apps disabled in the settings are skipped. Some editors may lose formatting when text is replaced.

Which languages does it support?

On Linux, choose Polish, English or German, either alone or as a pair for translation. macOS supports Polish and English. The quality of results depends on the model.

How much does it cost?

The app is free. You only pay for a paid external service, if you choose one.
