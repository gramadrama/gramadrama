How it works

Three moves, without leaving the window

Select text or place the caret in a supported field. Compatibility depends on the app and how it exposes its text.

  1. Write where you always do

    In a messenger, email or editor. The GramaDrama extension supports text fields in your browser.

  2. Ask for a correction

    Ctrl+Alt+G on Linux, ⌥⌘G on macOS. The panel shows proposed changes.

  3. Review and apply

    Enter inserts the result; Esc closes the panel. Read the suggestion — the model can be wrong.

Your data

Your text and voice, on your terms

No GramaDrama account is needed. Text goes to the configured model and to LanguageTool if enabled. For dictation, the selected speech service processes your recording.

Your servicesText: a model and optional LanguageTool. Recording: Whisper. Locally or on a server you choose.
  • No account, no telemetry. The app collects no statistics, and its logs do not contain the text being corrected.
  • Local after setup. Start the services you need and download their models first. The app has no default project server.
  • You choose the service. Choose a compatible model and services running locally or remotely. A remote service handles your data according to its operator’s policies.

Requirements and availability

What you need

Install and run your chosen models and services yourself, then point GramaDrama to them in its settings. They can run on your computer or your own server. You can also choose a compatible external service.

FAQ

What people ask

Is this an alternative to Grammarly and Wispr Flow?

GramaDrama combines two use cases: the proofreading and rewriting familiar from Grammarly, and the dictation familiar from Wispr Flow. Features and supported apps differ, and dictation is still experimental. Both can run locally once you set up the models on compatible hardware.

Does it work in my app?

Compatibility depends on how the app exposes text. The browser extension handles fields on web pages. Password fields, terminals and apps disabled in the settings are skipped. Some editors may lose formatting when text is replaced.

Which languages does it support?

On Linux, choose Polish, English or German, either alone or as a pair for translation. macOS supports Polish and English. The quality of results depends on the model.

How much does it cost?

The app is free and will be open source. You only pay if you choose a paid external service.

Is my text stored anywhere?

GramaDrama’s logs do not contain the text being corrected. Text goes to your chosen model and enabled LanguageTool during correction and background checking. If you use a server, data retention depends on its operator. Remote dictation also sends your recording to the speech service.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after downloading models and setting up local services. The model and any enabled LanguageTool or Whisper service must run on your computer or an accessible local network.

When can I download it?

The Linux version comes first, once testing is done; macOS later. Follow the progress on GitHub.
